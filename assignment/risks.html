Risk visualisation

The register's likelihood × impact data, two ways. Choose a view — both are computed live from the same data, including any risks added from the threat-intelligence lookup below.

A 5×5 likelihood × impact matrix. Each cell lists the risk IDs scored at that position; the band (score = likelihood × impact: 15+ high, 8–12 medium, 6 or less low) is conveyed by the cell's position and score as well as its colour.

The same data as an SVG bubble chart: bubble position shows likelihood and impact, bubble size shows how many risks share that position.

Live threat-intelligence lookup

This is the prototype's genuine third-party API integration. Recent identifiers are loaded from CIRCL into a datalist, after which the selected CVE is enriched through the CIRCL CVE Search API and the FIRST EPSS API. A resulting decision-aid can be added directly to the browser-persisted risk register.

Format: CVE-YYYY-NNNN. Try CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell).
